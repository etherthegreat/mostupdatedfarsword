## 1. UNDELIVERED · SOLDIER UNKNOWN · BUNK MATE UNKNOWN
*(yours — a dying soldier's dictated letter · classification: UNDELIVERED)*

In the time since I saw you last I've lost both hands. My bunk mate (hi!) dictates.

I miss you more than tongue can tell my darling. In my dreams I hold you, my mind refusing to let me dream in peace without humiliation. Who slipped you from this world just beyond my reach?

The redcoats beats their drums and I matched my tune to meet them, but in the aftermath of the conflict, i couldn't find who i had fought for.

I had a family once. maybe they're still here, maybe somewhere else. don't much matter to me as long as wherever I go next, i might see your face again.

## 2. PERSONAL EFFECTS, WARD 6 — clerk's intake log
*(companion draft · classification: DECLASSIFIED)*

Nobody warns you the letters keep coming after the men stop.

My work is the property of the dead — I empty the pockets, I catalogue, I try to find where it all goes home. Watches, mostly. Bibles. Photographs curled soft from body heat. And letters. God, the letters — half of them to people no registry can find: to wives who moved on, to children long evacuated, to the dead written by the dead.

I keep one drawer for the ones I can't send. It is the fullest drawer in the building.

I read them. I know I shouldn't. But a letter no one can receive is a sound no one will hear, and I decided long ago that I would at least be the ear. Today's had no name on it — the man couldn't sign, and whoever held the pen for him didn't either. Only a darling, already gone somewhere he seemed in a great hurry to follow.

I stamped it UNDELIVERED and laid it in the drawer with the others. Four hundred and eleven, now. This war does not make widows so much as it makes silence — and it has made me its clerk.

## 3. ARMY DEPARTMENT OF PERSONAL RECORDS — "Mental Health Day"
*(yours — the pre-Ualani corporate government, first war · classification: DECLASSIFIED)*

**MEMO — to all employees of the ADPR · Subject: Mental Health Support**

To all our department's incredible clerks and keepers.

It's come to the department's attention that recent news from the front has resulted in detrimental emotional effects on many of our staff. As a pro-mental illness department of the US government, it is our honor to announce Mental Health Day!

Tomorrow, before lunch, the department will host an 'Airing of Grief' event hosted by team leadership. Hopefully, those on the staff who have become bogged by letters from America's brave heroes will be able to share their load with the rest of the team — let's go team!

Attendance is mandatory.

— Management

**re: mental health day**

Following recent events, management has decided to cancel all scheduled Airing of Grief sessions.

Separately we will be conducting one on one performance reviews momentarily.

— management
